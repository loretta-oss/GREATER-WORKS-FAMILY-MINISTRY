Who We Are

Rooted in Faith, Dedicated to Family

Greater Works Family Ministry is a recognized nonprofit organization dedicated to fostering spiritual growth, positive youth development, marital strengthening, and community welfare.

Our Mission

To equip families and individuals with spiritual resources, emotional support, and practical guidance to overcome life’s challenges and thrive in purpose.

Our Vision

A united community where every household experiences holistic health, deep spiritual connection, generational healing, and unwavering mutual support.

Core Values

Compassion without judgment, integrity in all operations, steadfast community empowerment, and Christ-centered servant leadership.
